Part 6 • Explain Your Design Choices

My Color Analysis

Explain how your color choices relate to color theory and how you would use each palette in a website.

Palette 1

Harmony: Complementary

Why I selected these colors: I liked this random combination of brown, dark green, orange, and teal to create a strong contrast. The colors give the palette a natural and earthy appearance while the orange adds a noticeable pop of color.

Mood: This palette creates a bold, warm, and natural mood. The darker colors give the design a more serious feeling, while the orange adds energy and visual interest.

Website Usage: I would use the primary brown color for the header and major sections, the dark green for supporting elements, and the orange accent for buttons and important links. The teal background could be used to separate sections of the website.

Palette 2

Harmony: Analogous

Why I selected these colors: This randomized warm colors work closely together to create a consistent color scheme. The orange, red, and golden tones give the palette a unified appearance without having strong differences between the colors.

Mood: This palette creates a warm, energetic, and creative mood. The orange and red tones make the design feel active and welcoming.

Website Usage: I would use the primary orange color for the header and main sections, with the secondary orange for navigation and supporting elements. The red accent could be used for buttons, links, and other important elements, while the golden colors could be used for backgrounds and decorative areas. But I would use more darker hues of some colors since it is very hard to use together.

Palette 3

Harmony: Triadic

Why I selected these colors: I selected purple, green, and beige tones to create a balanced and creative color combination. The different colors give the palette variety while still working together as a single design.

Mood: This palette creates a calm, friendly, and creative mood. The lighter green and purple colors make the design feel softer, while the darker brown provides contrast.

Website Usage: I would use the purple as the primary color for the header and major sections, with green for supporting elements and navigation. The beige accent could be used for buttons and highlights, while the light green background would keep the main content area soft and welcoming. This was my favorite color palette of the 3!
